Privacy architecture

Built to leave you alone

Local only

Everything happens on your device. There’s no login, no sync, and no server waiting on the other end. Close the tab and the session is gone.

Auto-pause

Switch tabs or let the screen sleep and the session stops on its own. It never runs at you — it waits for you to come back.

No streaks, badges, or reminders. The app doesn’t want you back tomorrow. It’s done when you feel done.

The method

A steady rhythm,
left to right

Bilateral stimulation is a simple pattern: your attention is guided from one side of your body to the other, over and over, at a steady pace. Therapists use versions of it inside EMDR, a structured therapy for processing difficult memories. BMDR is not that. It borrows the rhythm — not the clinical protocol — and gives you a quiet place to use it on your own terms: to settle before sleep, to come down after a hard conversation, to give a racing mind one predictable thing to hold.

Worth saying plainly

BMDR is a practice tool, not treatment. It isn’t a medical device and it doesn’t replace care. If you’re carrying something heavy, that work belongs in a room with a professional.

Tap anywhere to pause. Sit for a breath before you go. And if life interrupts mid-session, BMDR pauses itself and waits.

The 87 Hz audio layer

The tone is built from two voices around an 87 Hz core, and its loudness shifts between your ears to match the anchor’s position.

What it does

Two channels.
One steady rhythm.

Gentle visual pacing

A single glowing anchor sweeps the screen at the pace you set. Follow it with your eyes — that side-to-side tracking is the entire visual mechanic. No score, no timer pressure, no interface in your way.

Calming stereo frequencies

Optional audio pans a low 87 Hz base tone — layered one octave below and one above — between your left and right ear. Link it to the visual rhythm or pace it independently. Off by default; one tap to invite it in.

Everything adjustable

Pace, anchor size, flow pattern, color, sound — every parameter lives in one bottom sheet, adjustable mid-session without breaking the rhythm. Your practice, tuned to the day you're actually having.

Private by design

A quiet room, not a platform.

No account

Open it and begin. There is nothing to sign up for and no profile following you around.

No install

Runs in the browser on phone or desktop. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like a native app.

Sound is opt-in

Audio starts muted, always. The frequencies only play when you switch them on.

Pauses with you

Switch tabs or lock your phone and the session pauses itself. It waits; it never runs without you.

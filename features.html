The controls

Every dial, documented.

No mystery settings, no locked menus you have to discover. This is the complete control surface of the practice space — exactly what each one does and exactly how far it goes.

Visual engine

What your eyes follow

Visual Pace

How many full left–right passes the anchor makes per second. Slow it to a drift or push it to a brisk sweep — adjustable in 0.1 steps, live, mid-session.

0.2 – 3.0 Hz · default 1.2
Visual Anchor Size

The size of the glowing focal point. Small and precise for tight visual tracking, large and soft when you want the motion to fill more of your field of view.

20 – 150 px · default 60
Flow Pattern

Gentle Sway is a clean horizontal line — the classic bilateral sweep. Infinite Flow traces a continuous figure-eight, adding a soft vertical rise and fall to the rhythm.

2 patterns
Soothing Palette

Four anchor colors, each with its own glow: teal, pink, purple, and pure white.

4 palettes
Audio engine

What your ears follow

Holistic Frequencies

A low 87 Hz base tone layered with one octave below and one octave above — a warm, full-bodied hum rather than a clinical beep.

87 Hz base · 3 layers
Left–Right Panning

The tone crosses smoothly between your left and right ear in rhythm — the audio half of the bilateral mechanic. Headphones recommended; not required.

True stereo
Link Rhythms

Locks the audio pace to the visual pace so eyes and ears move as one. Unlink it and the audio takes its own slider with the same 0.2–3.0 Hz range.

Linked or free
Protected Output

The audio graph runs through a compressor so levels stay smooth and even — no sudden spikes in your ears. Sound is off by default, always.

Off by default
The space itself

Interface that gets out of the way

The screen is the button

Tap anywhere to begin, tap anywhere to rest. No hunting for a tiny play icon while you're trying to settle.

One sheet for everything

Every adjustment lives in a single bottom sheet that slides over the session and slides away — settings never become a second app.

Status at a glance

A single quiet readout — Stillness or in motion — announced to screen readers as it changes. That's all the chrome there is.

Built for phones

Respects notches and home-bar safe areas, works in the browser, and can be added to your home screen to run full-screen like a native app.

Accessible by intent

Labeled controls, keyboard access, a skip link, and live status announcements — the practice space works with assistive tech, not around it.

Pauses when you leave

Switch apps or lock the screen and the session stops on its own. Nothing plays to an empty room; nothing drains your battery behind your back.

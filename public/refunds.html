Last updated July 14, 2026.

Approved refunds disable purchase-backed browser access. Mandatory consumer rights always control where they apply.

Digital product policy

BMDR is a digital product delivered through browser access and a downloadable file. Except where applicable law requires otherwise, completed digital purchases are generally final after access or download has been supplied.

Requests reviewed

BNDR LLC will review requests involving a duplicate purchase, an incorrect charge, or a material technical failure that prevents the purchaser from using BMDR as described and cannot be reasonably corrected. Submit the request through the Gumroad receipt or the contact method at bndrllc.com, preferably within 14 days of purchase.

Required information

Include the purchase email, Gumroad receipt or transaction reference, device and browser, and a concise description of the problem. Do not publish the license key in a public message.

Access after refund or dispute

A refund, payment dispute, chargeback, or disabled license makes the purchase ineligible for the access system. Existing signed access sessions may be invalidated or allowed to expire.

Gumroad and applicable law

Checkout terms, Gumroad’s processing rules, and mandatory consumer protections may provide additional rights or procedures. Those rights are not limited by this page.

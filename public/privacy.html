Privacy Notice

Last updated July 14, 2026.

BMDR does not collect or upload the content of your practice session. Purchase verification is separate from session activity.

1. Session activity

The movement, pace, color, pattern, audio state, and other session interactions run in your browser. The BMDR application does not send the content of a practice session to BNDR LLC.

2. Purchase verification data

When you unlock browser access, you submit the email used for purchase and the Gumroad license key. The verification endpoint sends those values to Gumroad solely to confirm the purchase and eligibility state. The application code does not intentionally store the submitted email or license key after the request completes.

3. Access cookie

After successful verification, BMDR sets a signed, expiring, HttpOnly cookie named bmdr_access. It records authorization and expiration, not the purchase email, license key, or session content. The default duration is seven days and may be configured up to thirty days. Deleting site cookies or using the sign-out action removes local access and requires verification again.

4. Hosting and standard logs

The hosting provider and Gumroad may receive ordinary network information such as IP address, request time, browser information, and request metadata under their own policies. The verification code avoids logging request bodies, purchase emails, and license keys. Provider-operated security and infrastructure logs may be retained according to provider settings.

5. External resources

BMDR currently requests Google Fonts and a BNDR LLC image hosted through a Railway service. Those providers may receive normal request information. Gumroad processes checkout and purchase records under Gumroad’s privacy terms.

6. Analytics and advertising

The supplied deployment adds no analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral profiling, or sale of personal information.

7. Retention and requests

BNDR LLC does not maintain a BMDR user account through this package. Purchase records remain with Gumroad. Questions or legally available privacy requests may be submitted through the contact method at bndrllc.com or the Gumroad receipt.

8. Changes

This notice may be updated when hosting, verification, or data practices materially change. The updated date appears at the top of this page.

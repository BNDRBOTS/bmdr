Support

Last updated July 14, 2026.

Already purchased

Open the BMDR access page and enter the purchase email and the license key from the Gumroad receipt. The two values must belong to the same eligible purchase.

License not accepted

  • Copy the complete license key without added spaces.
  • Use the exact email address shown on the Gumroad receipt.
  • Confirm the purchase was not refunded, disputed, chargebacked, or disabled.
  • Wait briefly and retry if Gumroad or the hosting service is temporarily unavailable.

Audio

Audio begins only after direct interaction because browsers restrict autoplay. Use headphones for clear left-right movement, begin at a low device volume, and stop if the sound is uncomfortable.

Browser access

Current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge with JavaScript, Canvas 2D, cookies, and the Web Audio API enabled are expected. Private-browsing settings or blocked cookies may require verification each visit.

Sign out or reset access

Use the sign-out control on the access page or delete cookies for the BMDR site. This does not affect the Gumroad purchase.

Contact

Use the support or contact method in the Gumroad receipt or at bndrllc.com. Include the browser, device, and exact error message, but do not send the license key through an insecure or public channel.

FAQ

Straight answers.

No dodging, no fine-print games. If your question isn't here, the fastest answer is thirty seconds inside the practice space itself.

Is BMDR therapy or EMDR? +

No. BMDR is a self-guided wellness tool that delivers bilateral stimulation — the rhythmic left–right mechanic used in EMDR-style practice. It is not medical treatment, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it is not a substitute for working with a licensed therapist. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services.

Do I need to install or sign up for anything? +

No. BMDR runs in your browser on phone or desktop — open it and begin. There is no account, no profile, and no notification permission request. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it runs full-screen like a native app.

What exactly does the audio do? +

A low 87 Hz base tone, layered one octave below and one octave above, pans smoothly between your left and right ear in rhythm. You can lock it to the visual pace with Link Rhythms or give it its own independent pace. The output runs through a compressor so levels stay even — and it's off by default, every session, until you turn it on.

What's the difference between the two flow patterns? +

Gentle Sway is a clean horizontal sweep — the classic side-to-side line. Infinite Flow traces a continuous figure-eight, adding a soft vertical rise and fall while keeping the same left–right rhythm. Both use the same pace, size, and palette settings.

How fast should I set the pace? +

The pace slider runs from 0.2 to 3.0 Hz — full left–right passes per second — and opens at 1.2. Start at the default, then adjust toward whatever feels comfortable to follow. There is no correct number; the slider exists precisely so you can find yours, and you can change it mid-session without stopping.

What happens if I switch apps or lock my phone? +

The session pauses itself immediately. BMDR never keeps running — or keeps playing sound — when you're not looking at it. When you come back, you resume with a tap.

What's free and what's paid? +

The personal practice is free in your browser — the real engine, not a demo. The full practice is unlocked through Gumroad secure checkout via the Unlock Full Practice button. Payment is handled entirely by Gumroad; BMDR never sees your card details.

Who makes BMDR? +

BMDR (BEMDR) is built and maintained by BNDR LLC — independently, with no ad network, no data brokering, and no engagement metrics to feed. Unlocking the full practice directly supports that independence.
